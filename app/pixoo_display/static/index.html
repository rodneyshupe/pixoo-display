Cities

The clock shows each city's weather and its local time, one after another in this order. With more than one, a short frame with the city's 3-letter code comes first (edit the code in the box). Untick a city to skip it (it stays in the list). Drag a row by its ⠿ handle (or use ▲ ▼) to reorder.

Weather from Open-Meteo (free, no account), refreshed every 10 minutes.

Display

The Countdown mode (switch to it above) shows each countdown that is on, one after another: its short name, then the days, hours or minutes left.

Countdowns

A one-off countdown stays for the day it happens, then drops out. A yearly one counts down to its next date (birthdays, holidays). The code (up to 4 letters) is the short name shown on the Pixoo.

Timer

The Pixoo's own timer: it shows the countdown on screen and beeps when it reaches zero, then the usual programme comes back.
